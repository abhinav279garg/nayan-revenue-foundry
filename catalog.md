## Delivery

After a successful payment, the receive-only Stripe webhook matches the purchased product to its approved release and creates a time-limited download link for the exact tested ZIP. No credentials, customer files, or private runtime data are stored in this repository.

## Scope

These are small utilities for defined workflows, not a promise of suitability for every dataset. Read the product description and included README before use. Examples are synthetic; product validation is deterministic and local-first.
